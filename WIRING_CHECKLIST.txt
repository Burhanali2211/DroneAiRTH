WIRING CONNECTIONS
──────────────────
☐ Pixhawk Telemetry 2 Port (6-pin JST connector on side of FC)
  ├─ Pin 2 (TX) [white/gray wire] ──→ RPi Pin 10 (GPIO 15, RX)
  ├─ Pin 3 (RX) [black wire]       ──→ RPi Pin 8 (GPIO 14, TX)
  └─ Pin 6 (GND) [brown wire]      ──→ RPi Pin 6 (GND)

☐ UART Serial Wiring Check:
  ├─ Pixhawk TX → RPi RX (cross-wired, NOT straight)
  ├─ Pixhawk RX → RPi TX (cross-wired, NOT straight)
  └─ GND → GND (same potential reference)

☐ DO NOT connect:
  ├─ Pixhawk +5V to RPi +5V (RPi self-powered)
  ├─ Pixhawk CTS to anything (optional flow control, leave floating)
  └─ Pixhawk RTS to anything (optional flow control, leave floating)
